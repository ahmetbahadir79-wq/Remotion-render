Deep Work by Cal Newport proves that knowledge work has spent thirty years confusing frantic network connectivity with actual economic value.

A rigorous analysis of "Deep Work" by Cal Newport: exploring the economic pressures rewarding deep focus, Sophie Leroy's attention residue experiments, the four distinct depth philosophies, and the strict shutdown rituals required to reclaim cognitive sovereignty.

Best for professionals, knowledge workers, creators, and students who want actionable frameworks to produce high-value output without burning out in open-office distractions.
