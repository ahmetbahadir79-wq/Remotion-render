## Description

You step in quicksand. Every instinct screams THRASH — kick, grab, fight your way out. And that instinct is exactly what pulls you under. Russ Harris says your anxiety works the same way.

This is a deep dive into "The Happiness Trap" by Russ Harris and the science of Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT). The core argument is unsettling: the pursuit of happiness is itself the trap. Everything we're taught to do with painful feelings — fight them, fix them, distract, think positive — is the thrashing that tightens the quicksand. This is the opposite of positive thinking.

We walk through the whole framework in plain language: the Struggle Switch and why the struggle (not the feeling) is the trap, why a normal healthy mind manufactures suffering, why controlling your emotions backfires, and the ACT skills that actually work — defusion, expansion, the observing self, values as a compass, and committed action. It ends on the reframe that changes everything: real happiness was never about feeling good.
